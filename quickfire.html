~18 min

1 × 10 reps · slow spinal mobilisation
💪 Main
Plank Hold
3 × 30s · pelvic tilt first · 30s rest
Hollow Body Hold
1 × 60s · ribs locked to pelvis · pelvic tilt throughout

3 × 8 each side · pelvic tilt · 40s rest
Heel Taps
3 × 15 reps · pelvic tilt · 30s rest
Crunches
3 × 15 reps · arms crossed · exhale up · 30s rest

Spinal Twist — Left

Spinal Twist — Right
30s · drop right knee across, shoulders flat
Seated Fold
30s · legs straight, reach toward feet
Key: 🔑 Posterior pelvic tilt before every exercise. If you feel it in your neck or hip flexors — reset the tilt.

Arm Circles
30s · forward then backward

30s · across chest and out wide
Slow Push-Up
5 reps · very slow, joint primer only
💪 Main
Push-Ups
3 × 10 reps · pelvic tilt · 60s rest
Bottom Push-Up Hold
3 × 10s max effort · squeeze hard · 30s rest
Shaolin Wall Push
6 × 10s max effort · 10s rest between

Upward Salute
30s · sweep arms overhead, reach tall
